# Donations and support

The UI includes a disabled “Support the project” placeholder. No payment processor, donation account or tax claim is configured. A future provider must sit behind a small configuration/abstraction and must never block reporting.

Before enabling contributions, decide the responsible legal entity, payment provider, fees, refund handling, accounting, privacy terms, fraud controls, reconciliation, currency and whether any tax-deductibility statement is legally accurate. Do not describe the project as a registered charity/PBO unless that becomes true.
